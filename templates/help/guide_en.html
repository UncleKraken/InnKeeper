Getting started

InnKeeper runs in a web browser. Reception, the restaurant, housekeeping and the office can all use it at the same time, on computers, tablets or phones connected to the same server.

  1. Just looking? On the first setup screen, Try it with sample data fills InnKeeper with a sample hotel, restaurant, bar and spa. Nothing in it is real; the yellow banner at the top has Start with my own business, which removes it all (your own staff accounts stay) and starts the setup.
  2. Set up your business. The first time a manager signs in, the setup wizard asks what kind of business you run (hotel, guesthouse, or restaurant/bar) and creates rooms and outlets for you. Run it again any time from Settings.
  3. Add your staff under Staff. Each person gets their own login and a role. They only see the parts of the app their role needs.
  4. Fill in your menu under Outlets & menus → Menu & services, and arrange your tables with Floor plan.
  5. Check rooms and rates under Rooms & rates (hotels and guesthouses).
  6. Add your logo and receipt text under Settings → Receipts & invoices.
  7. Download a backup under Settings → Backup & restore, and keep it somewhere safe.
Which role should each person have?
  • Manager – everything, including settings, staff, voids and discounts.
  • Reception – room rack, reservations, guests, check-in/out, guest bills, housekeeping board and the POS.
  • Service staff – waiters, bartenders, spa staff: the POS only. You can limit them to certain outlets in the outlet's settings.
  • Housekeeping – cleaning board, tasks and maintenance reports.
  • Maintenance – maintenance tickets.
  • Finance – reports, day close, expenses, ledger and guest list.
Changing the language

Use SQ / EN at the bottom of the menu. Each person's choice is remembered. The default for new users is set in Settings → Business details.

Front desk

Making a reservation

On the Room rack, click an empty day in a room's row, or press New reservation. Search for a returning guest by name, phone or email, or type a new guest's name. Leave the rate empty to use the room type's standard price.

InnKeeper refuses double bookings and too many guests for the room type.

Checking in and out

Arrivals for today are on the Dashboard. Press Check in. A warning appears if the room is not clean yet.

To check out, open the reservation and press Check out. Room nights are added to the bill automatically. If something is still owed, take the payment first (cash, card or bank transfer). A manager can check out with an open balance, for example for company invoices; it then appears under Unpaid bills in Finance.

After check-out the room is marked dirty and a cleaning task is created for housekeeping.

Guest bill (folio)

Everything the guest owes is on one bill: room nights, extras you add, and restaurant, bar or spa orders charged to the room. Use a negative payment for a refund. Mistakes are voided by a manager with a reason; nothing is ever deleted.

Invoice opens a printable invoice. It gets its own invoice number when the bill is settled.

Moving a guest to another room

Open the reservation and use Move room. Only rooms free for the whole stay are listed. If the guest is already in house, the old room goes to housekeeping.

Cancellations and no-shows

Open the reservation and press Cancel reservation or No-show. The room becomes free again.

Nightly room charges

Every night InnKeeper adds the previous night's room charge to each in-house guest's bill, so daily revenue reports are correct. If it ever doesn't run, nothing is lost: check-out always adds any missing nights.

Prices, seasons & online booking

Seasonal prices

In Rooms & rates → Seasons add periods such as Summer or New Year. A season either changes every room type by a percentage (+30 for 30% more, −15 for a discount) or sets a fixed nightly price for one room type. You can also require a minimum number of nights: the booking page refuses shorter stays, and at reception InnKeeper asks you to tick Book it anyway first (it is noted in the activity log).

Leave the rate empty when making a reservation and InnKeeper prices each night from the price list. A stay that crosses into a season shows the price per night, and the bill gets one line per price.

Taking bookings on your website

Turn it on in Settings → Online booking. Guests choose dates, see the free room types with photo, description and total price, and send their details. Add photos and English texts in Rooms & rates → Room types, and untick Bookable online for rooms you don't want to sell online.

Copy the link or the ready-made Book now button into your website, Instagram or Google profile. Guests can only open it when InnKeeper is reachable from the internet; set the public web address once it is.

Confirming online requests

New requests appear on the dashboard and under Reservations → Online requests, and as striped bars on the room rack. The room is held for them straight away. Press Confirm, or Decline with an optional message; the guest gets an email either way. To confirm automatically, untick Confirm each online booking by hand.

If you ask for a deposit, the guest sees your bank details and the booking code to use as the payment reference. Online payment by card is not taken.

Email

In Settings → Email enter your mail provider's details so guests get confirmations and reception gets an alert for every new booking. For Gmail use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, STARTTLS and an app password. Press Send test email to check.

Groups, channels & guest register

Group bookings

Under Reservations → Groups → New group booking, enter the group name, the contact person and the dates, press Show free rooms, tick the rooms and press Book the group. Leave the group rate empty to use the price list.

With One bill for the whole group the main room pays for everyone: when another room checks out, its nights and extras move to the main room's bill. The group page checks in or checks out all rooms with one button and can cancel the whole group. Change each room's guest with the edit button.

Channel manager (recommended for several channels)

Booking.com, Airbnb and Expedia give their connection only to certified partners, so InnKeeper connects through Channex, a certified channel manager. Open an account at channex.io, create your property with the same room types and connect your channels there. Then in Rooms & rates → Channel manager enter the Channex API key and property ID, tick Connect to the channel manager and link each room type to its Channex room type and rate plan.

From then on InnKeeper sends the number of free rooms, the price of every night (room rates and seasons) and minimum stays whenever something changes, and fetches new, changed and cancelled bookings every minute. They appear on the room rack with the guest's name and the channel's price. If a booking can't be placed (no free room, or a room type that isn't linked), it is listed in red on that page and on the dashboard instead of being overbooked. Try it first with Use the Channex test system. When you use the channel manager, you don't need calendar links for the same rooms.

Calendar links (simple, one room at a time)

In Rooms & rates → Calendar links each room has an InnKeeper calendar link. Paste it into the channel's calendar import (Booking.com: Rates & availability → Sync calendars; Airbnb: Availability → Connect calendars) so rooms booked here close there.

Then copy the channel's export link and add it to the room in InnKeeper. Its bookings appear on the room rack, changed dates are updated and cancellations are cancelled. The Windows app checks every 15 minutes; Sync all now checks immediately. If a channel booking falls on a room that is already taken, InnKeeper does not overbook: it shows a red warning so you can move one guest.

Calendar links carry only dates. Add the guest's name and check the price when the booking arrives.

Guest register for the authorities

Guests → Guest register lists everyone who stayed on the chosen days with nationality, date of birth and document. Tick Foreign guests only if that is what you must report, then print it or download it for Excel. Guests with missing ID details are highlighted; InnKeeper also reminds you at check-in.

Card payments

InnKeeper can take deposits and bills by card through POK (Albanian, accepts businesses with a NUIS, ALL and EUR) or Paysera. Open a business account with one of them, then enter its keys in Settings → Card payments and keep Test mode on until you have tried it. InnKeeper never sees card numbers: the guest pays on the provider's secure page.

Deposits from the website

With Let guests pay the deposit by card, the booking confirmation page shows a Pay now button next to your bank details. When the payment is confirmed it appears on the guest's bill as Online card payment and reception gets an email.

Payment links from reception

On any reservation, Card payment by link creates a link for a deposit or the bill. Email it to the guest or copy it into WhatsApp. If a confirmation doesn't arrive, Check asks the provider again; a manager can Mark paid after checking the provider's app.

Payment confirmations come over the internet, so the public web address (Settings → Online booking) must be set and reachable.

Fiscalization (tax authority)

When Settings → Fiscalization is switched on, InnKeeper sends every paid restaurant/bar bill and every closed guest bill directly to the tax authority (DPT), signed with your electronic seal certificate. The receipt and the invoice then show the NSLF, the NIVF and a QR code the customer can scan to check it. Room nights use the accommodation VAT rate; food, drinks and other services use the main VAT rate.

Setting it up
  1. Apply on e-Albania for the electronic seal certificate for fiscalization and download the .p12 file and its password.
  2. In the tax authority's self-care portal register the business unit (the premises), the software (InnKeeper as your own software) to get the software and maintainer codes, each cash register (TCR) and each operator.
  3. In Settings → Fiscalization enter the codes, upload the certificate, tick Fiscalize receipts and invoices and keep Use the tax authority's test system on.
  4. Give every cashier their operator code in Staff → edit. An outlet with its own cash register gets its TCR code in Outlets & menus → edit.
  5. Make a few test sales. Under Finance → Fiscal they must show Registered with an NIVF. Then switch the test system off.
No internet

Sales never stop. Without a connection the receipt is printed with its NSLF and the note that the NIVF will be added later. InnKeeper sends waiting documents every minute once the connection returns; Finance → Fiscal → Send waiting sends them immediately. The law allows 48 hours: the dashboard warns you when a document gets close to the limit.

Cash in the drawer

The opening cash is reported automatically before the first cash sale of the day (the amount counted at the last day close). With several cash registers, set each outlet's own change under Outlets & menus → edit → cash in this register each morning; the main register reports the rest, so the same money is never reported twice. When cash is taken out of the drawer, report it under Finance → Fiscal → Cash in the drawer.

Voids and rejected documents

Voiding a fiscalized receipt sends a correction invoice that cancels it. If the tax authority rejects a document, it is shown in red with the reason (usually a wrong code or an expired certificate). Fix the setting and press Send again.

Restaurant, bar & services

Taking an order

Open Restaurant, bar & services and choose an outlet. Tap a table on the floor plan (busy tables are highlighted with their total), then tap items to add them. Use − and + to change quantities. Search finds items by name in either language.

For services without tables (spa, laundry, takeaway), type a name and press New order.

Taking payment, change and splitting the bill

Choose Cash, Card or Room. For cash, enter what the customer gave you (or tap a quick amount) and InnKeeper shows the change to give back.

To split a bill, use ÷2, ÷3 or ÷4, or type any amount, and take several payments, even mixing cash and card. The table closes when everything is paid.

The printer button pays and prints the receipt in one step.

Charging to a guest's room

Choose Room, pick the in-house guest and confirm. The order appears on their bill and the receipt has a signature line.

Discounts

Use the % tab. Enter a percent or an amount and a reason. Managers can give any discount; service staff only up to the limit set in Settings → Receipts & invoices.

Moving or merging tables

Use Move to table… at the top of the order. Choosing a free table moves the order; choosing a busy table merges both orders into one.

Receipts, reprints and voids

Every bill gets a receipt number. Receipts lists them all; open one to reprint it. A manager can void a closed receipt with a reason. It stays visible as VOID, and its sales are removed from reports.

Sold-out items

In Outlets & menus → Menu & QR, press Mark sold out. The item disappears from the POS and the guest menu until you make it available again.

Shared tills, PINs & bills in English

Shared tills: switch with a PIN

On the bar tablet or any device several people use: sign in as a manager, open Settings → Shared tills & PINs and press Use this device as a shared till. From then on it opens on a screen with everyone's name: each person taps their name and types their PIN. Lock / switch user (at the bottom of the menu, or Switch at the top on a phone) hands the till to the next person, and it locks itself after a few minutes without a tap (set the minutes on the same page). Every sale is recorded under whoever is signed in.

Each person sets their own PIN under the grid icon next to their name (their password is asked), or a manager sets it under Staff → edit → PIN. 4 to 6 digits; easy ones like 1234 are refused, and five wrong tries lock that person for 15 minutes. Office computers and personal phones stay normal devices with username and password.

Printing the bill in English

Next to Print bill on an order there is EN: it prints the same bill in English, with the English names of the menu items when you filled them in. On a receipt or a guest invoice on screen, choose Shqip or English at the top before printing.

Kitchen screen sound and “ready” alerts

The kitchen and bar screens beep and flash on every new order, and beep again once a minute while an order is waiting for more than two minutes. Browsers only allow sound after a first tap, so after opening the screen tap the yellow button Tap here to turn on the sound. Waiters get a chime and a green banner on the table plan when an order is ready to serve.

Using a phone

Waiters, housekeeping and reception can use their own phones on the business Wi-Fi. Open the address shown in the InnKeeper window on the main computer (for example http://192.168.1.20:8765) and sign in.

  • Add it to the home screen so it opens like an app: Android Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen; iPhone Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Taking orders: the bar at the bottom switches between Menu and the Bill and shows the total. Tap items to add them (a short message confirms each one); the menu stays where you were. Send sends new items to the kitchen and bar.
  • Payment: open the Bill, choose cash, card or room and take the money, the same as on the computer. Moving the table and printing the bill are under More at the bottom of the bill.
  • Housekeeping opens on the rooms that need cleaning.

Floor plan

Open Outlets & menus and press Floor plan next to an outlet (or Edit floor plan on the POS screen).

  • Move a table by dragging it. Resize it from its bottom-right corner. Arrow keys nudge the selected table.
  • Click a table to rename or renumber it, change seats or shape (square, round, long), or move it to another area.
  • Areas such as Inside, Terrace or Garden each get their own plan. Double-click an area's name to rename it.
  • Tidy up lines all tables up neatly. Duplicate copies the selected table.
  • Press Save plan when you're done. Tables with open orders can't be removed.

Kitchen & bar screens, printers

How orders reach the kitchen

In Outlets & menus → Stations you have places where food and drinks are prepared, such as Kitchen and Bar. For each menu category choose Prepared at. Items from categories without a station (a coffee made at the counter, a spa treatment) are served directly.

New items on an order are marked new. Press Send to send them; anything not yet sent is also sent automatically when the bill is paid. Adding more of an item that was already sent creates a new line for the kitchen; removing one shows it crossed out on the kitchen screen.

The kitchen screen

Open Kitchen & bar screens on a tablet or TV and choose the station. Tickets appear by themselves with a sound, oldest first, with the time waiting. Tap Start and then Ready; the table gets a green dot on the waiter's floor plan. Recall brings back the last ticket cleared by mistake.

Give cooks and bartenders the role Kitchen & bar: they see only the screens.

Receipt and ticket printers

Add thermal printers (58 or 80 mm, ESC/POS) in Outlets & menus → Printers: a network printer by its IP address, or a USB printer installed on the computer running the Windows app. Press Test print to check.

Choose a receipt printer for each outlet (bills, receipts and the cash drawer) and a ticket printer for each station. Without a printer, receipts print from the browser as before. Failed prints are listed under Recent print jobs with a Retry button.

Menu & QR code

Each outlet has a guest menu that opens on a phone by scanning a QR code. Guests can switch between Albanian and English. Add English names and descriptions to your items to show them in English.

In Outlets & menus → Menu & QR you can print the menu on A4 paper and print QR table cards.

Note: guests' phones must be able to reach your InnKeeper address, so the QR code only works when InnKeeper is published on the internet or your guest Wi-Fi.

Options, happy hour, accounts & shifts

Item options (sizes, sugar, extras)

In Outlets & menus → Options create a group such as Size (Small / Large +0.50), Sugar (No sugar / Little / Normal) or Extras (Milk +0.20, Cream +0.30, several allowed), and tick the items it goes with. Tick Must choose when the waiter has to pick one (e.g. a size). On the till, those items open a short list of choices; the choices are printed on the kitchen ticket and the receipt, and the price changes by itself.

Happy hour and late-night prices

In Outlets & menus → Happy hour add a time-based price: a name (shown on the bill), the change in % (−20 for 20% cheaper, +15 for 15% more), the hours and days, and optionally which outlets and categories. A rule from 23:00 to 03:00 runs past midnight and belongs to the evening it started. While it runs, the till shows the old price struck through and the new one.

Customer accounts (regulars who pay later)

A manager opens Customer accounts → New account with the name, phone, an optional credit limit and anything the customer already owed (from the notebook). On the till, choose Account, type the name and put the bill on the account. The sale counts as revenue that day; the money counts when it is paid. Payments are taken on the customer's page (cash, card or bank transfer), which also shows the full statement and can be printed. Staff can't go over the credit limit; a manager can.

Loyalty cards (every 10th coffee free)

Switch them on in Settings → Loyalty & gift vouchers and choose how many stamps give a free item. Mark the items that earn a stamp (Outlets & menus → item → earns a loyalty stamp), e.g. coffees. At the till, type the customer's phone or card number above the payment buttons (or New card with their name and phone). When the bill is paid, each marked item adds a stamp. With enough stamps the till shows Give the free item: the cheapest marked item on the bill becomes free and the stamps start again. A voided bill takes its stamps back. Loyalty cards in the menu lists everyone with their stamps and visits.

Gift vouchers

Switch them on in Settings → Loyalty & gift vouchers. Gift vouchers → Sell a gift voucher: type the value and how it was paid, then print the voucher with its code. The money counts in today's cash or card, but it is not a sale yet. To spend it, choose the gift icon among the payment buttons and type the code: it pays as much of the bill as it covers, the rest is paid another way, and what's left on the voucher can be spent on another visit. Vouchers expire after the months you set (0: never). A manager can void a voucher that hasn't been used. Fiscal receipts show the voucher as the payment type “SVOUCHER”; ask your accountant to confirm this fits how you sell vouchers.

Shifts and tips

Staff press Start shift (on the outlets page or under My shift) with the cash they start with. Every payment they take is counted in their shift. At the end they count the cash and press End shift: InnKeeper shows the cash expected and the difference, and the report can be printed. Managers see all shifts under Finance → Shifts.

Tips: for a card payment type the tip in Tip; for cash, tick Guest leaves it as a tip when the guest leaves the change. Tips are not revenue. Card tips are paid out to the staff member from the cash, so the day close and the shift take them off the cash expected.

Table bookings, club tables & entrance

Table bookings

On the tables screen press Bookings, then New booking: name, day, time and number of people are enough; phone, table and a note are optional. Two bookings can't overlap on the same table (a booking lasts two hours unless you change it). A booked table shows Reserved 20:00 · Name (6) on the floor plan from three hours before. When the guests arrive press Seat: their bill opens on the table with their name and number of people. If nobody comes, press No-show.

Club tables with a minimum spend

Fill in Minimum spend on the booking (e.g. 200 for a VIP table). While that table's bill is open, the till shows how much is still missing. At the end, if they spent less, press Add the difference: one line with the missing amount is added and the bill reaches the minimum.

Entrance tickets and one-tap sales

For a door, ticket desk or takeaway counter, open the outlet in Outlets & menus and tick One-tap sale. Add the items (e.g. Entrance 10, VIP entrance 20). The outlet's screen then shows big buttons: choose Cash or Card once, and every tap sells one and takes the payment at once (with a receipt, and fiscalized when that is on). Each button counts how many were sold tonight.

Housekeeping & maintenance

The Housekeeping board shows every room by floor: clean, dirty, inspected or out of order, plus who is staying and who arrives today. Tap Mark clean when a room is done.

Tasks are created automatically at check-out; reception or a manager can add more and assign them. Housekeeping staff press Start and then Done.

Anyone can Report a problem. Ticking room out of order blocks the room from being sold until the ticket is resolved.

Finance

Reports

Reports shows revenue by day and department, expenses, profit, payments by method, unpaid guest bills and, for hotels, occupancy, average rate (ADR) and RevPAR for any date range.

Closing the day (Z report)

At the end of the day open Day close. Enter the cash that was in the drawer in the morning and the cash you count now. InnKeeper works out what should be there (morning cash + cash received − cash paid out) and shows any difference. Print the Z report for your records.

Tomorrow's starting cash is filled in from today's count.

Expenses

Record purchases, salaries, bills and other costs under Expenses so reports show real profit. Cash expenses are taken into account in the day close.

Sales report

Best-selling items, sales by staff member, average bill and busiest hours for your restaurant, bar and services.

Ledger and Excel

The Ledger lists every charge and payment. Excel downloads a real Excel file (.xlsx) for your accountant, with money as numbers and a filter on every column; CSV is there too. The sales report, the stock list and the guest register download to Excel the same way.

Month report for the accountant

Finance → Month report puts the month on one page: sales by department, VAT by rate, money received by payment method, expenses by category, profit, checks (bills, discounts, voids, cash differences, fiscal documents) and every day of the month with its cash count. Choose the month, then Print / PDF (choose “Save as PDF” in the print window) or Excel. Early in a month it opens on the month before.

Daily summary by email

In Settings → Email, under Daily summary for the owner, type one or more email addresses and the hour. Every morning InnKeeper emails how the day before went: sales compared with a week ago, money by payment method, the cash count and any difference, discounts, voids, cancelled bills, shifts with a cash difference, best sellers, rooms, and warnings (stock running low, receipts not yet fiscalized, copies not working). It is also sent right after the day close. Days with no sales send nothing. Send now shows you how it looks.

Stock & inventory

Getting started

Open Stock and add what you buy: ingredients, bottles, cleaning supplies, room amenities, each with its unit (kg, litre, bottle, piece…) and a reorder level. For drinks sold by the bottle, the quickest way is Recipes → Track as stock, which creates the stock item and links it to the menu item in one click.

Recipes: sales take stock automatically

On the Recipes tab, say what one sale of each menu item uses, in the stock item's unit: 0.25 kg of lamb for a tavë kosi, 0.04 l of raki for a shot, 1 bottle of beer. Every paid bill or room charge then takes those amounts out of stock; a voided receipt puts them back. The tab also shows each dish's cost and margin.

Deliveries, waste and counts

Record delivery adds what arrived with the price per unit from the invoice; InnKeeper keeps an average purchase price and can add the total to Expenses. Record breakage or spoiled goods on the item's page. Count the shelves regularly with Count stock (print the count sheet first): differences are saved as count corrections, so losses show up.

The Usage report shows what was used, the cost of goods sold against your sales (cost of sales %), waste and count losses for any period. Items at or below their reorder level appear on the dashboard.

Profit per item

Stock → Profit per item shows for every menu item what it costs to make (its recipe at average purchase price), its price without VAT, the margin, how many were sold and the profit in the period. Items under 60% are marked red: raise the price or check the recipe. Items without a recipe show add recipe. Sort by profit, margin or quantity, or download it to Excel.

Orders to suppliers

Stock → Orders → Order what is running low prepares one order per supplier with every item at or below its reorder level, enough to get back to twice the level. Change the quantities, then send it: WhatsApp opens the chat with the order already written, Email sends it if the supplier has an email address, or Print / Copy as text for Viber or SMS. Mark it as ordered. When the goods arrive, It arrived: receive opens a delivery filled in from the order: correct what actually came and save, and the stock goes up.

Settings & backups

  • Business details – name, NIPT, address, currency, VAT, default language and when the business day ends.
  • Receipts & invoices – logo, header and footer text, 58 or 80 mm paper, VAT breakdown, staff discount limit. A preview shows the result.
  • Modules – switch areas on or off. Switched-off areas disappear for everyone; no data is deleted.
  • Backup & restore – a full backup holds everything and is used to move to a new computer; settings only copies your setup (rooms, outlets, menus, tables) without guests or money, for a second location.
Moving InnKeeper to a new computer
  1. On the old computer: Settings → Backup & restore → Download full backup.
  2. Install InnKeeper on the new computer and sign in with the first account it asks you to create.
  3. Settings → Backup & restore, choose the file and press Check file, then confirm by typing RESTORE.
  4. Sign in again with your usual account.
Automatic copies to a USB drive or the cloud

The Windows app saves a full backup on this computer every day and keeps the last 30. But if the computer breaks or is stolen, those are gone too. In Settings → Backup & restore → Automatic copies to another place choose a folder on a USB drive or in OneDrive / Google Drive / Dropbox (install their app and sign in first) and how often. InnKeeper writes a full copy there, reads it back to check it, and keeps every copy from the last two days, one per day for two months and one per month after that. A password encrypts the copies (recommended for the cloud; write it down).

If the copy stops working (USB drive unplugged, cloud folder gone), the dashboard shows a red warning. To restore, the list under the settings shows the copies with a Restore button; on a new computer, install InnKeeper, choose the same folder and restore the newest copy.

Activity log

Settings → Activity log records every check-in, payment, discount, void and setting change, with who did it and when.

Using InnKeeper from home or on the go

Settings → Access from outside. For the owner, the safe way is Tailscale: a free app on this computer and on your phone, signed in with the same account. Your phone then reaches InnKeeper privately from anywhere, and nothing is opened to the internet; the page shows the address and a code to scan. For the online booking page and card payments, which need a public https address, paste a Cloudflare Tunnel token there (free Cloudflare account and a domain name); InnKeeper keeps the tunnel running. From outside, sign-in always needs the username and password: PINs work only inside the business.

Import from Excel

Settings → Import from Excel brings in your menu, stock list or guest list. Download the Excel template (the second sheet explains each column), fill it in with one row per item, save it and upload the .xlsx file. CSV files work too. A preview shows what will be added or updated and which rows have problems; nothing is saved until you confirm. Entries with the same name are updated, nothing is deleted. Albanian column names work too.

Go-live checklist

Before the first real guest, on the Windows PC at the property:

  1. The PC – connect it by cable to the router, set Windows power options so it never sleeps, and turn off automatic restarts for updates during opening hours. Give it a fixed address in the router so tablets and kitchen screens always find it.
  2. Business details – name, NIPT, address, VAT rates and the receipt header/footer. Print a test receipt.
  3. Rooms, rates and seasons – check every room type, price and season; make a test reservation, check it in and out, then cancel or void it.
  4. Menus and stock – import them from Excel (Settings → Import from Excel), then check prices on a printed receipt.
  5. Staff – one account per person with the right role and, for cashiers, their fiscal operator code. Never share the manager account.
  6. Printers and screens – print a test page on every receipt and kitchen printer; open the kitchen/bar screens on their devices.
  7. Fiscalization – test system first, a few sales registered, then switch to live (see Fiscalization).
  8. Online booking, card payments and channel manager – these need InnKeeper to be reachable from the internet. Set the public web address in Settings → Online booking (for example through a Cloudflare Tunnel or your router's port forwarding with HTTPS). Use each provider's test mode first.
  9. Backups – download a full backup and copy it to a USB drive or cloud folder; repeat every week. The app also keeps 30 daily backups on the PC.
  10. First day – at closing do a day close and count the cash, so the next day's opening cash is right.

Windows app & updates

The Windows installer adds an InnKeeper icon. Opening it starts InnKeeper in the background and opens it in your browser. Other devices on the same Wi-Fi can use it too: the address is shown in the InnKeeper window.

When a new version is available, managers see a notice at the top of the screen. Open Settings → Updates and press Update now: InnKeeper saves a backup, downloads the update from GitHub, checks it and installs it. It closes for about a minute and opens again by itself. Windows may ask to allow changes: choose Yes.

“Windows protected your PC” when installing

InnKeeper is free and its installer isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows doesn't know the publisher yet. It is safe if you downloaded it from the official page (github.com/UncleKraken/InnKeeper/releases).

  1. In the browser, if it asks, choose Keep (Edge: … → Keep → Show more → Keep anyway).
  2. In the blue Windows window, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. If the antivirus blocks it, restore it from quarantine and allow it; programs made with Python are sometimes flagged by mistake.

Updates from inside InnKeeper don't show this warning.

Questions

Someone forgot their password

A manager opens Staff, chooses the person and presses Set a new password. Passwords can't be viewed, only replaced.

"Too many failed attempts"

After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask a manager to set a new password.

Is the receipt a fiscal receipt?

Yes, when fiscalization is switched on: it shows the NSLF, NIVF and QR code. Without it, receipts say “This is not a fiscal receipt.” See Fiscalization.

Can I delete a payment or a bill?

No, on purpose: money records are never deleted. A manager voids them with a reason, which keeps the history honest and complete.

We are open after midnight

In Settings → Business details set Business day ends at to the hour after your last guests leave (for example 04:00). Sales, payments, expenses and stock until that hour then count on the night they belong to, so the whole night is in one day close, and the day close opened at 02:00 shows that night. Room nights and fiscal receipts keep the real calendar date. Businesses set up as Night bar, club or lounge start at 05:00.

We are not registered for VAT, or not registered at all

In Settings → Business details choose the Tax registration: Registered, with VAT (VAT is shown on receipts and invoices); Registered, without VAT (small businesses: no VAT lines, the NIPT and fiscalization stay available); or Not registered (associations, places outside Albania, internal use or trying the app): no NIPT, VAT or fiscalization anywhere, guest bills are called receipts and every receipt says “Internal receipt”. In Albania a business selling to the public usually needs a NIPT and fiscal receipts, so ask your accountant if you are not sure.
