Getting started

InnKeeper runs in a web browser. Reception, the restaurant, housekeeping and the office can all use it at the same time, on computers, tablets or phones connected to the same server.

  1. Set up your business. The first time a manager signs in, the setup wizard asks what kind of business you run (hotel, guesthouse, or restaurant/bar) and creates rooms and outlets for you. Run it again any time from Settings.
  2. Add your staff under Staff. Each person gets their own login and a role. They only see the parts of the app their role needs.
  3. Fill in your menu under Outlets & menus → Menu & services, and arrange your tables with Floor plan.
  4. Check rooms and rates under Rooms & rates (hotels and guesthouses).
  5. Add your logo and receipt text under Settings → Receipts & invoices.
  6. Download a backup under Settings → Backup & restore, and keep it somewhere safe.
Which role should each person have?
  • Manager – everything, including settings, staff, voids and discounts.
  • Reception – room rack, reservations, guests, check-in/out, guest bills, housekeeping board and the POS.
  • Service staff – waiters, bartenders, spa staff: the POS only. You can limit them to certain outlets in the outlet's settings.
  • Housekeeping – cleaning board, tasks and maintenance reports.
  • Maintenance – maintenance tickets.
  • Finance – reports, day close, expenses, ledger and guest list.
Changing the language

Use SQ / EN at the bottom of the menu. Each person's choice is remembered. The default for new users is set in Settings → Business details.

Front desk

Making a reservation

On the Room rack, click an empty day in a room's row, or press New reservation. Search for a returning guest by name, phone or email, or type a new guest's name. Leave the rate empty to use the room type's standard price.

InnKeeper refuses double bookings and too many guests for the room type.

Checking in and out

Arrivals for today are on the Dashboard. Press Check in. A warning appears if the room is not clean yet.

To check out, open the reservation and press Check out. Room nights are added to the bill automatically. If something is still owed, take the payment first (cash, card or bank transfer). A manager can check out with an open balance, for example for company invoices; it then appears under Unpaid bills in Finance.

After check-out the room is marked dirty and a cleaning task is created for housekeeping.

Guest bill (folio)

Everything the guest owes is on one bill: room nights, extras you add, and restaurant, bar or spa orders charged to the room. Use a negative payment for a refund. Mistakes are voided by a manager with a reason; nothing is ever deleted.

Invoice opens a printable invoice. It gets its own invoice number when the bill is settled.

Moving a guest to another room

Open the reservation and use Move room. Only rooms free for the whole stay are listed. If the guest is already in house, the old room goes to housekeeping.

Cancellations and no-shows

Open the reservation and press Cancel reservation or No-show. The room becomes free again.

Nightly room charges

Every night InnKeeper adds the previous night's room charge to each in-house guest's bill, so daily revenue reports are correct. If it ever doesn't run, nothing is lost: check-out always adds any missing nights.

Prices, seasons & online booking

Seasonal prices

In Rooms & rates → Seasons add periods such as Summer or New Year. A season either changes every room type by a percentage (+30 for 30% more, −15 for a discount) or sets a fixed nightly price for one room type. You can also require a minimum number of nights.

Leave the rate empty when making a reservation and InnKeeper prices each night from the price list. A stay that crosses into a season shows the price per night, and the bill gets one line per price.

Taking bookings on your website

Turn it on in Settings → Online booking. Guests choose dates, see the free room types with photo, description and total price, and send their details. Add photos and English texts in Rooms & rates → Room types, and untick Bookable online for rooms you don't want to sell online.

Copy the link or the ready-made Book now button into your website, Instagram or Google profile. Guests can only open it when InnKeeper is reachable from the internet; set the public web address once it is.

Confirming online requests

New requests appear on the dashboard and under Reservations → Online requests, and as striped bars on the room rack. The room is held for them straight away. Press Confirm, or Decline with an optional message; the guest gets an email either way. To confirm automatically, untick Confirm each online booking by hand.

If you ask for a deposit, the guest sees your bank details and the booking code to use as the payment reference. Online payment by card is not taken.

Email

In Settings → Email enter your mail provider's details so guests get confirmations and reception gets an alert for every new booking. For Gmail use smtp.gmail.com, port 587, STARTTLS and an app password. Press Send test email to check.

Restaurant, bar & services

Taking an order

Open Restaurant, bar & services and choose an outlet. Tap a table on the floor plan (busy tables are highlighted with their total), then tap items to add them. Use − and + to change quantities. Search finds items by name in either language.

For services without tables (spa, laundry, takeaway), type a name and press New order.

Taking payment, change and splitting the bill

Choose Cash, Card or Room. For cash, enter what the customer gave you (or tap a quick amount) and InnKeeper shows the change to give back.

To split a bill, use ÷2, ÷3 or ÷4, or type any amount, and take several payments, even mixing cash and card. The table closes when everything is paid.

The printer button pays and prints the receipt in one step.

Charging to a guest's room

Choose Room, pick the in-house guest and confirm. The order appears on their bill and the receipt has a signature line.

Discounts

Use the % tab. Enter a percent or an amount and a reason. Managers can give any discount; service staff only up to the limit set in Settings → Receipts & invoices.

Moving or merging tables

Use Move to table… at the top of the order. Choosing a free table moves the order; choosing a busy table merges both orders into one.

Receipts, reprints and voids

Every bill gets a receipt number. Receipts lists them all; open one to reprint it. A manager can void a closed receipt with a reason. It stays visible as VOID, and its sales are removed from reports.

Sold-out items

In Outlets & menus → Menu & QR, press Mark sold out. The item disappears from the POS and the guest menu until you make it available again.

Floor plan

Open Outlets & menus and press Floor plan next to an outlet (or Edit floor plan on the POS screen).

  • Move a table by dragging it. Resize it from its bottom-right corner. Arrow keys nudge the selected table.
  • Click a table to rename or renumber it, change seats or shape (square, round, long), or move it to another area.
  • Areas such as Inside, Terrace or Garden each get their own plan. Double-click an area's name to rename it.
  • Tidy up lines all tables up neatly. Duplicate copies the selected table.
  • Press Save plan when you're done. Tables with open orders can't be removed.

Kitchen & bar screens, printers

How orders reach the kitchen

In Outlets & menus → Stations you have places where food and drinks are prepared, such as Kitchen and Bar. For each menu category choose Prepared at. Items from categories without a station (a coffee made at the counter, a spa treatment) are served directly.

New items on an order are marked new. Press Send to send them; anything not yet sent is also sent automatically when the bill is paid. Adding more of an item that was already sent creates a new line for the kitchen; removing one shows it crossed out on the kitchen screen.

The kitchen screen

Open Kitchen & bar screens on a tablet or TV and choose the station. Tickets appear by themselves with a sound, oldest first, with the time waiting. Tap Start and then Ready; the table gets a green dot on the waiter's floor plan. Recall brings back the last ticket cleared by mistake.

Give cooks and bartenders the role Kitchen & bar: they see only the screens.

Receipt and ticket printers

Add thermal printers (58 or 80 mm, ESC/POS) in Outlets & menus → Printers: a network printer by its IP address, or a USB printer installed on the computer running the Windows app. Press Test print to check.

Choose a receipt printer for each outlet (bills, receipts and the cash drawer) and a ticket printer for each station. Without a printer, receipts print from the browser as before. Failed prints are listed under Recent print jobs with a Retry button.

Menu & QR code

Each outlet has a guest menu that opens on a phone by scanning a QR code. Guests can switch between Albanian and English. Add English names and descriptions to your items to show them in English.

In Outlets & menus → Menu & QR you can print the menu on A4 paper and print QR table cards.

Note: guests' phones must be able to reach your InnKeeper address, so the QR code only works when InnKeeper is published on the internet or your guest Wi-Fi.

Housekeeping & maintenance

The Housekeeping board shows every room by floor: clean, dirty, inspected or out of order, plus who is staying and who arrives today. Tap Mark clean when a room is done.

Tasks are created automatically at check-out; reception or a manager can add more and assign them. Housekeeping staff press Start and then Done.

Anyone can Report a problem. Ticking room out of order blocks the room from being sold until the ticket is resolved.

Finance

Reports

Reports shows revenue by day and department, expenses, profit, payments by method, unpaid guest bills and, for hotels, occupancy, average rate (ADR) and RevPAR for any date range.

Closing the day (Z report)

At the end of the day open Day close. Enter the cash that was in the drawer in the morning and the cash you count now. InnKeeper works out what should be there (morning cash + cash received − cash paid out) and shows any difference. Print the Z report for your records.

Tomorrow's starting cash is filled in from today's count.

Expenses

Record purchases, salaries, bills and other costs under Expenses so reports show real profit. Cash expenses are taken into account in the day close.

Sales report

Best-selling items, sales by staff member, average bill and busiest hours for your restaurant, bar and services.

Ledger and Excel

The Ledger lists every charge and payment. Export CSV opens in Excel or Google Sheets for your accountant.

Settings & backups

  • Business details – name, NIPT, address, currency, VAT and default language.
  • Receipts & invoices – logo, header and footer text, 58 or 80 mm paper, VAT breakdown, staff discount limit. A preview shows the result.
  • Modules – switch areas on or off. Switched-off areas disappear for everyone; no data is deleted.
  • Backup & restore – a full backup holds everything and is used to move to a new computer; settings only copies your setup (rooms, outlets, menus, tables) without guests or money, for a second location.
Moving InnKeeper to a new computer
  1. On the old computer: Settings → Backup & restore → Download full backup.
  2. Install InnKeeper on the new computer and sign in with the first account it asks you to create.
  3. Settings → Backup & restore, choose the file and press Check file, then confirm by typing RESTORE.
  4. Sign in again with your usual account.
Automatic backups

The Windows app saves a full backup every day and keeps the last 30. On a server, schedule python manage.py backup. Copy backups to another place (USB drive, cloud) regularly.

Activity log

Settings → Activity log records every check-in, payment, discount, void and setting change, with who did it and when.

Windows app & updates

The Windows installer adds an InnKeeper icon. Opening it starts InnKeeper in the background and opens it in your browser. Other devices on the same Wi-Fi can use it too: the address is shown in the InnKeeper window.

When a new version is available, managers see a notice at the top of the screen. Download and run the new installer: your data stays in place, and a backup is made before the update.

Questions

Someone forgot their password

A manager opens Staff, chooses the person and presses Set a new password. Passwords can't be viewed, only replaced.

"Too many failed attempts"

After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask a manager to set a new password.

Is the receipt a fiscal receipt?

No. InnKeeper's receipts and invoices are internal documents. In Albania, sales must also be fiscalised through a certified fiscal service or device.

Can I delete a payment or a bill?

No, on purpose: money records are never deleted. A manager voids them with a reason, which keeps the history honest and complete.
